
# Task 1 – Key Findings and Business Insights

## 1. Overview

The Daikibo telemetry analysis provided a visual understanding of equipment downtime across multiple factories and device types.

## 2. Key Analytical Areas

### Factory-Level Analysis

The factory-wise downtime visualization provided a comparison of equipment downtime across different factory locations.

It helped identify variations in operational performance between factories.

### Device-Level Analysis

The device-type-wise downtime visualization provided insights into how downtime was distributed across different equipment categories.

It helped identify device categories that may require additional operational attention.

### Interactive Analysis

The dashboard filter enabled factory-level exploration.

Selecting a factory dynamically updated the device-type visualization, making it easier to examine equipment performance at individual locations.

## 3. Business Insights

- Monitoring equipment downtime can help organizations understand operational inefficiencies.
- Factory-level comparisons can support maintenance prioritization.
- Device-level analysis can help identify equipment categories requiring further investigation.
- Interactive dashboards make operational information easier to explore and communicate.
- Telemetry data can support data-driven maintenance and operational decisions.

## 4. Recommendations

- Monitor equipment health regularly.
- Investigate factories showing comparatively higher downtime.
- Examine device categories associated with frequent unhealthy events.
- Use telemetry analytics to support preventive maintenance.
- Continue monitoring operational performance through dashboards.

## 5. Conclusion

The task demonstrated how industrial telemetry data can be transformed into meaningful visual insights using Tableau.

The dashboard provided a structured approach to exploring equipment downtime and understanding operational performance across factories and device types.
